# Activity 5: Exploring and Improving AI Prompts

## Activity Description

This activity focused on exploring how artificial intelligence tools respond to different prompts and how improving a prompt can affect the quality of the generated output. We first selected an AI tool and created an original prompt related to our chosen topic. After saving the initial output, we used a leading LLM to refine and improve our original prompt. We then entered the improved prompt into the same AI tool, saved the new output, and compared both results based on their clarity, detail, accuracy, and usefulness.

## What Have I Learned?

Through this activity, I learned that the way we write and structure our prompts can greatly influence the responses generated by AI tools. I gained a better understanding of prompt engineering and how providing clearer instructions, specific details, and proper context can produce more relevant and useful results. One challenge I encountered was identifying how to improve my original prompt to get a better response, which I addressed by using another LLM to refine and reformulate my instructions. Comparing the two outputs also helped me recognize the importance of evaluating AI-generated information rather than immediately accepting the first response. In the future, I can apply these skills to make better use of AI tools for academic tasks, research, and other activities while continuing to improve my ability to create effective prompts.
