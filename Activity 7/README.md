<h1 align="center"> 📄 Activity 7: Resume Generating

## Activity Description

This activity focused on exploring and comparing the resume-generation capabilities of two different Large Language Models (LLMs), namely Claude AI and ChatGPT. We created one prompt containing the instructions and details needed to generate a resume and entered the exact same prompt into both AI tools. After generating the two resumes, we examined and compared their outputs based on their structure, clarity, formatting, and overall quality. This activity allowed us to explore how different AI tools interpret the same instructions and produce different results.

## What Have I Learned?

Through this activity, I learned how Large Language Models can assist in creating professional documents, particularly resumes. I gained a better understanding of how AI tools such as Claude AI and ChatGPT can generate different outputs even when given the same prompt. One important thing I learned was that the quality of an AI-generated resume depends not only on the information provided but also on how the AI tool organizes and presents that information. Comparing both resumes helped me recognize their differences in structure, clarity, formatting, and overall quality. In the future, I can apply what I learned when creating or improving my own resume and become more effective in using AI tools for document preparation. I also learned the importance of reviewing and editing AI-generated content to ensure that it is accurate, appropriate, and professionally presented.
